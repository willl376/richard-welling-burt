Sources & Links

About This Site

The Civil War letters, poems, and diary pages reproduced here were originally published as the site “The Civil War Letters and Poems of Richard W. Burt,” © 1995–2000 by Larry Stevens, Newark, Ohio, hosted at my.ohio.voyager.net/~lstevens/burt/. That host has long since gone offline. The full site was preserved here from the Internet Archive Wayback Machine and rebuilt as a modern static site, with the addition of Burt's collected War Songs, Poems and Odes, the history of his regiment, and related archival material.

Primary Sources

  • Original site on the Wayback Machine — The Civil War Letters and Poems of Richard W. Burt (snapshot May 14, 2008).
  • War Songs, Poems and Odes (1906) — Captain R. W. Burt, J. W. Franks & Sons, Peoria, Illinois, 1906. Scanned copy from the Princeton University Library collection, digitized by Google, at the HathiTrust Digital Library. Full-text PDF: warsongs_poems_and_odes_burt_1906.pdf.
  • John J. Metzgar Papers (#4939) — Southern Historical Collection, Wilson Special Collections Library, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Finding aid: finding-aids.lib.unc.edu/04939. Contains the typed history of the 76th Ohio Volunteer Infantry transcribed on the 76th Ohio page.
  • Charles Dana Miller narrative — the diary accounts on this site are found in A Narrative of the Services of Brevet Major Charles Dana Miller in the War of the Great Rebellion 1861-1865, transcribed by Roy Gilman Miller, Tennessee State Library and Archives, Nashville.
