The John J. Metzgar Papers

The John J. Metzgar Papers (Collection #4939) are held by the Southern Historical Collection, Wilson Special Collections Library, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. John J. Metzgar served in the 76th Ohio Volunteer Infantry — the same regiment as Captain Richard W. Burt — and his papers are a rich companion to Burt's letters, poems, and diary.

What the Collection Contains

  • Series 2, Folder 9 (History): a typed history of the 76th Ohio Volunteer Infantry, written by one of its officers, covering the regiment from its organization at Newark, Ohio in February 1862 through the Grand Review in Washington in May 1865. This history is transcribed in full on the 76th Ohio page of this site.
  • Handwritten letters: scans of cursive 1860s correspondence. These pages (the remaining ~257 pages of the digitized file) are handwritten and have not yet been transcribed.

Reading the Handwritten Letters

The cursive handwriting of the 1860s does not read well with ordinary OCR. If you would like to work with these letters, the practical options are:

  • Transkribus — a free-tier platform purpose-built for transcribing historic handwriting. This is the best tool for the job.
  • Google Lens (phone app) — quick reads of individual pages.
  • UNC Wilson Library research services — staff can provide or recommend transcriptions.

Source: John J. Metzgar Papers (#4939), Southern Historical Collection, Wilson Special Collections Library, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Finding aid: https://finding-aids.lib.unc.edu/04939/
